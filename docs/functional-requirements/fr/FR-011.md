# FR-011: Report Aggregate Latency And Plugin Failure Counts

- **Status:** Active
- **Traces to:** [`US-07`](https://github.com/ITPD-Almo/modular-llm-gateway/issues/67), [`CON-04`](../../product-vision.md#con-04)
- **Containers:** `gateway`
- **Changed:** 2026-10-25: fixed the future metrics endpoint, units, reset rule, denominator and label bound; allowed measurement tolerance for the real-time comparison.

## Behavior

The gateway shall expose a read-only JSON metrics response at `/metrics`, counting each chat attempt once and each terminal failed or timed-out hook once, and reporting hook duration sums in milliseconds and terminal-hook counts.
The mean hook duration shall be the sum divided by the count for the same plugin, stage and outcome; a zero count shall have no mean rather than divide by zero.
The counters shall be cumulative for one gateway application process and reset when that process restarts; multiple server processes shall not be presented as a combined total.

The gateway shall label hook figures only by plugin, stage and outcome, and never by prompt, response, credential or request identifier.
Plugin labels shall have at most 32 values including a single overflow label, stages shall be request/response and terminal outcomes completed/failed/timed_out, yielding at most 192 hook series.

Implementation status: future target, not implemented by the current diagnostics module. [ADR-006](../../architecture/adr/ADR-006.md) selects parent-process aggregation; [QR-07](../../quality-requirements/qr/QR-07.md) makes its resource bound checkable.

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC-01

Given a synthetic workload of 10 normal, 3 failing and 2 timed-out requests in one window, when the figures are read, then they report 15 requests, 3 plugin failures and 2 plugin timeouts.

### AC-02

Given requests carrying synthetic secret strings, when the figures are read, then every label is a plugin, a stage or an outcome and none of the strings appears.

### AC-03

Given equal-size runs with a healthy plugin and the same plugin delayed by 200 ms while remaining under its hook deadline, when their mean hook durations are compared, then the delayed mean is at least 180 ms higher; controlled-clock unit checks verify the exact sum/count calculation independently of scheduling noise.

### AC-04

Given nonzero counters, when the application process restarts, then all totals begin at zero; 10,000 synthetic requests through eight configured plugins do not create per-request metric series.
